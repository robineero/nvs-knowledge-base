## Before creating content

**Look what has been done so far.** Google the keywords and see which content ranks and think why. If you want to rank then you need to compete with already ranking content (kick somebody out from the first page). Which means your content needs to be (probably) somewhat similar but much better.

**Write down all the keywords** (for example "cholesterol") and phrases ("how to measure cholesterol") that come to your mind around your topic. Then, let's look at the search volume on your market by using [Google Keyword Planner](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7337243). This way you know before writing which words and phrases people use to find information around the topic.
